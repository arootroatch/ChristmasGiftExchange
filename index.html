Free Online Secret Santa & Gift Exchange Generator

A free Secret Santa generator that doesn't want anything from you

Draw names online for your family, friends or office. There are no ads and no tracking, and there's no account or password to create. Just add your names, keep couples and households from drawing each other, and choose how everyone finds out who they're buying for.

How to draw names online

  1. Add everyone's names. Group people who live together into households so they can't draw each other.
  2. Pick how to share the results. Send one link with Names from a Hat, email each person privately with Secret Santa Mode, or see the whole list at once with Quick Match.
  3. Let everyone find out. Each person sees only who they're buying for, unless you chose to see the whole list with Quick Match.

Made for the way your group actually does this

Family gift exchanges need rules that keep partners and housemates from drawing each other, even when everyone lives far apart. Office Secret Santa usually needs the opposite: one quick link, with no collecting your coworkers' email addresses.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads and no paid tier. If you'd like to, you can leave a tip through Buy Me a Coffee.

Do I need an account or an email address?

You never create an account or a password. Names from a Hat and Quick Match don't need any email addresses. Secret Santa Mode does, because it emails each person their recipient, and the organizer confirms their own address with a one-time code.

Can I stop couples and housemates from drawing each other?

Yes. Put people in the same household and they'll never draw each other. This works in all three modes.

How do anonymous wishlists work?

In Secret Santa Mode, each person can add wishlist links, specific items and shipping details. Whoever drew them can see all of it, and they never find out who that was. So you can buy the right thing, or mail it across the country, without giving away the secret.

Does it work for Kris Kringle, Pollyanna or Amigo Secreto?

Yes. They're all names for the same tradition, so you can use this to draw names for a Kris Kringle, a Pollyanna, an Amigo Secreto or any other gift exchange.

What information do you keep?

Only what's needed to run your exchange, and it's never sold. The privacy page lists exactly what's stored in each mode and for how long.
