Free Family Gift Exchange Name Generator

Drawing names from a real hat stops working once your family is spread across a few states. And when one person does the drawing for everyone, that person knows every match. This free generator handles the draw for you, keeps couples and households apart, and lets everyone find out on their own.

Draw names for your family

Keep households from drawing each other

Most family exchanges have one rule: you don't buy for the person you live with, since you already share a budget and a Christmas morning. Add each household as a group, and the generator won't match anyone with someone in their own group. Parents, couples and roommates all get matched with someone outside their home.

Three ways to run your family's draw

  • Names from a Hat: send one link to the family group chat. Each person opens it, picks their own name and sees who they're buying for. Nobody needs to share an email address. Anyone with the link could pick another person's name, so this works best in a family that trusts each other.
  • Secret Santa Mode: enter everyone's email address and each person gets a private email with their recipient. This is the mode to pick for wishlists and shipping details.
  • Quick Match: see the whole list right away and draw again until you're happy with it. It's a good fit when the matches don't need to be a secret, like when you're dividing up kids' gifts.

Share wishlists without giving away the secret

Guessing what your brother-in-law wants gets old. In Secret Santa Mode, each person can add wishlist links, specific items and a mailing address. Whoever drew them can see it all, and they never find out who that was. That also makes it easy to ship a gift straight to relatives who live far away.

Use it again next year

If you save your exchange, you can reuse the same list of people and households next year instead of typing it in again. You can also look up your past exchanges from the participant dashboard.

Family gift exchange questions

How many people can join?

Up to 100 people can join a Names from a Hat exchange, which is plenty for even a big extended family.

Do we all need accounts?

No. Nobody creates an account or a password. Names from a Hat and Quick Match don't need email addresses at all. In Secret Santa Mode, people use a one-time code sent to their email to reach their dashboard.

Is it really free?

Yes, with no ads and no tracking. You can read exactly what's stored for each mode.
