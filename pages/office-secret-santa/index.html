Free Office Secret Santa Generator

Organizing the office Secret Santa shouldn't mean collecting everyone's email address or asking your coworkers to sign up for another website. With this generator, you type in the names and send one link. There are no ads, no tracking and no accounts.

Send one link and you're done

Names from a Hat gives you one link to drop into Slack, Teams or an email to the team. Each coworker opens it, picks their own name and sees who they're buying for. Each name can only be picked once, and nobody has to hand over an email address. Even the organizer only sees their own match.

Anyone with the link could pick someone else's name, so it works on trust. If you'd rather not rely on that, use Secret Santa Mode instead, which needs email addresses.

Works for remote and hybrid teams

Since everything happens online, it doesn't matter who's in the office that week. For a team that mails gifts to each other, Secret Santa Mode lets people share a shipping address and wishlist with whoever drew them, and nobody finds out who drew them unless the giver tells them.

How to run an office Secret Santa

  1. Check who wants to join. Make it opt-in so nobody feels pressured to take part.
  2. Agree on a budget and a date. A clear spending limit keeps it comfortable for everyone.
  3. Draw names. Add everyone's names, and put close colleagues or couples in a group if they shouldn't draw each other.
  4. Share the link. Remind people to pick only their own name.
  5. Exchange gifts. Do the big reveal at the party, or keep it anonymous.

Office Secret Santa questions

Does the organizer get to play too?

Yes. With Names from a Hat or Secret Santa Mode, the organizer finds out only their own match, just like everyone else.

Do we have to give out work emails?

Not with Names from a Hat or Quick Match. Only Secret Santa Mode needs email addresses, because it sends each person their match privately.

Is this the same as Kris Kringle or Pollyanna?

Yes, those are other names for the same kind of exchange, so this works whichever name your office uses. You can see exactly what's stored for each mode.
